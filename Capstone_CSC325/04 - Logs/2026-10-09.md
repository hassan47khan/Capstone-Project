## Decisions

**`schema.yml` stays in `backend/`.** It is a generated OpenAPI document, not a
GitHub Actions workflow — Actions parses every `.yml` under
`.github/workflows/` as a workflow and would report it broken, and
`manage.py spectacular --file schema.yml` writes it relative to `backend/`. The
staleness *check* belongs in CI, and now lives there.

**Record CI figures with their provenance.** Notes say whether a number came
from a runner, a local run or a commit message, and when. A coverage percentage
with no source behind it is not evidence.
